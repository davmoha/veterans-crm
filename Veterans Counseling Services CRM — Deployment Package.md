## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create a Render Account
1. Go to [render.com](https://render.com)
2. Click "Sign Up" and create a free account
3. Verify your email

### Step 2: Deploy the CRM
1. Click the **Deploy to Render** button (provided separately)
2. Authorize the connection to GitHub
3. Select "Create New" for the database
4. Click "Deploy"
5. Wait 2-3 minutes for deployment to complete

### Step 3: Access Your CRM
1. Render will provide a URL (e.g., `veterans-crm.onrender.com`)
2. Click the link to open your CRM
3. Create your first admin account
4. Start adding team members

## 🔧 How to Use the CRM

### Adding Constituents

**Option 1: Manual Entry**
1. Go to **Constituents** page
2. Click **"Add Constituent"**
3. Fill in the form and click **Save**

**Option 2: CSV Import**
1. Go to **CSV Import** page
2. Download the template
3. Fill in your data
4. Upload the file
5. Review and confirm

### Searching & Mass Email

1. Go to **Constituents** page
2. Use **Advanced Filters** to find constituents by:
   - State (FL, Florida, etc.)
   - ZIP code
   - Area code
   - Contact type
   - Email opt-in status
3. Check boxes to select constituents
4. Click **"Send Email"**
5. Your email client opens with all selected emails in the To: field

### Managing Your Team

1. Go to **Users** page (admin only)
2. View all team members
3. **Promote/Demote** — Toggle admin roles
4. **Delete** — Remove team members
5. **Invite** — Add new team members by email

## 📞 Support & Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"I forgot my password"**
- Use the "Sign In" page to log in with your email
- If you can't access your account, contact your admin

**"The CRM is slow"**
- Render may be starting up (first request takes 30 seconds)
- Refresh the page
- Clear your browser cache

**"I can't upload a CSV file"**
- Check that your file is in `.csv` format
- Ensure all required columns are present
- See the CSV Import template for the correct format

**"I need to add more users"**
- Go to the **Users** page
- Click **"Invite New User"**
- Enter their email and share the invite link

### Getting Help

For technical issues:
1. Check the **QUICKSTART.md** file
2. Review the **README.md** for architecture details
3. Contact your organization's CRM administrator
